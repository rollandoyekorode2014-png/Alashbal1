CUB Admissions

We are delighted that you are considering Alashbal International School for your child's educational journey.

How to Join

Admission Process

Enquire

Contact us or visit the campus to learn more about AIS and available places for your child.

Tour

Schedule a school tour to experience our learning environment and meet the team.

Apply

Submit an application form with complete details of your child to our admission office.

Assessment

Once we have reviewed your application, we will schedule a written assessment test and parent interview with the child.

Offer

Successful applicants receive an email. Parents are requested to complete a reservation form and secure a seat with the reservation fee.

Enrollment

Complete enrollment formalities. The reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable but posted as registration fee.

What You Need

Required Documents

  • Recent passport-sized photo of your child
  • Child’s passport copy
  • Parent’s/guardian’s passport copy
  • Child’s birth certificate
  • Valid QID (for child and parents)
  • Child's Immunization Record
  • Child's recent report/progress card if applicable
  • Any other relevant reports relating to learning and/or medical requirements
