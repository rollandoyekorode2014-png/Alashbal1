About Alashbal International School

Committed to Education · Home of the Cubs

Founded in 2009, Alashbal International School has grown to become a vibrant educational community, proudly serving over 1,800 students. Alashbal International School was founded with one purpose: to build a space for all students to grow, learn and create with each passing day. Through a unique teaching approach and a truly passionate staff, we help students develop academically and personally to the highest level.

At AIS, we offer a diverse and dynamic curriculum designed to foster intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and a love for learning. Our dedicated faculty and staff, and comprehensive extracurricular programs provide our Cubs with a well-rounded educational experience that prepares them for future life.

We invite you to explore our site and discover the academics and community Alashbal International School provides to each and every student. Get in touch with us today to schedule a tour, learn more about enrollment, or ask any other questions.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

ALASHBAL International School (AIS) is committed to provide premium quality education and aims to develop the skills, talents, and intellectual intelligence of students as a solid foundation for future life.

Vision

ALASHBAL International School (AIS) envisions itself to be a prominent educational institution that is dedicated to nurture our brilliant YOUNG ACHIEVERS of today to be competitive global leaders of tomorrow.

Our Journey

Milestones

2009

Founded as a Pre-school with 37 pupils. The beginning of the AIS story – building a space for students to grow, learn and create.

2014

Primary school opened, expanding programmes from Nursery through Year 6.

Growth

Today we proudly serve over 1,800 students across Kindergarten, Primary and Preparatory levels as a vibrant educational community in Doha.

Today

Cambridge International School status and continued commitment to premium quality education for every Cub.

Leadership

Welcome Message

Welcome to Alashbal International School!

It is with great pleasure and pride that I extend a warm welcome to you all to our vibrant and dynamic learning community at Alashbal International School (AIS). As the founding School Administrator, I am honored to lead an institution that is dedicated to providing excellence in education, fostering a nurturing environment, and empowering every student to reach their full potential.

At AIS, we are committed to delivering a premium quality education that nurtures the intellectual intelligence, talents and skills of each student. Our rigorous academic programs are designed to inspire curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong love of learning. Through innovative teaching methods, and a diverse range of extracurricular activities and school events, we strive to cultivate well-rounded individuals who are prepared to thrive in an ever-changing global society.

Our dedicated team of educators, administrative staff and support services are passionate about creating an inclusive and supportive learning environment where every student feels valued, respected, and empowered to succeed. We believe in the power of collaboration, partnership, and community engagement, and we encourage active involvement from parents and stakeholders in shaping the educational experience of our students.

I am confident that together, we will achieve great things.

I invite you to explore our website, learn more about our programs and offerings, and discover the many ways in which you can become a part of our AIS family.

Thank you for choosing Alashbal International School as your partner in education.

I look forward to working closely with each of you to make your educational journey a truly enriching and rewarding experience.

Roaring load,
Ms. Kathreen Agno-D'Sena
Head of School
